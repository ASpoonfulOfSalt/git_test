Eggs Benedict

Description

These bacon wrapped asparagus spears are full of flavor! Perfect for a delicious springtime appetizer or side dish. Cook it on the grill or bake in a hot oven or air fryer for extra crispy bacon.

Ingredients

  • Asparagus
  • Olive Oil
  • 6 Slices of Bacon
  • Ground black pepper

Steps:

  1. Oil the asparagus
  2. Wrap the asparagus in bacon
  3. Cook
  4. Serve
